# ADR 0001 — scope and use cases

**Status:** accepted for bootstrap

PersonaLattice starts as an identity-intelligence and public-source research
system, not an automated employment/tenant/credit decision engine.

Why:

- the technical work is already difficult without mixing in regulated decision
  workflows;
- evidence quality matters before business scoring;
- privacy/purpose controls are easier to test when the permitted modes are
  explicit.

Allowed bootstrap purposes:

- self audit
- consented due diligence
- public-source research
- professional credential verification

Blocked bootstrap purposes:

- employment eligibility decision
- housing eligibility decision
- credit eligibility decision
- insurance eligibility decision
